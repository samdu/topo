# Topo roadmap

How the pieces fit together is in `README.md`, `docs/design.md` and the area docs it links; this file is what has been built, what is in hand, what comes next, and what is parked or was tried and left. Sam's decisions and the reasoning behind them are the wiki page [buddy-for-helen](https://github.com/samdu/memory/blob/main/wiki/buddy-for-helen.md) in `samdu/memory`; the process is `docs/process.md`. Defects are [this repo's issues](https://github.com/samdu/topo/issues); tests that fail now and then and pass on a rerun carry the [`flake`](https://github.com/samdu/topo/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aflake) label.

## Today

Topo is the phone app. Signed in through Claude Code's own login, the iPhone is the primary: the real Claude Code, pinned at 2.1.316, runs resident in an Alpine userland inside the app (iSH's arm64 emulator) with permissions bypassed, because the guest is the sandbox and what it reaches is the holes the app makes: the memory's folder mounted at `/home/topo/memory`, a loopback API proxy that passes Claude Code's own login through unchanged, a loopback tool service behind the `topo` command, a DNS forwarder onto the phone's resolver, and the internet directly. Every turn is written to the CloudKit log, which the watch, the TV, other iPhones and iPads as viewers, and Womble on old iPads read.

You talk to it by holding the jewel: Parakeet hears on the phone and Phonon reads the reply aloud as it streams, behind the lock. The memory is an Obsidian vault, local or in iCloud Drive, that the mind reads and writes. Through `topo` the mind reaches reminders, the calendar, notifications, contacts, location, HomeKit, Apple Maps, Photos, the file picker, a shared screen, AlarmKit timers, GitHub and 1Password, and writes the phone's widgets, the watch face's slots, Control Center controls and Live Activities. Things reach it from the share sheet and Shortcuts. A turn sent in front keeps running behind another app.

The Mac hub (`TopoHub`) holds the lease, shows the pairing code and serves the house's LAN surfaces; it runs no turns. Topo is on TestFlight with the internal group House and on Sam's phone; the external group Household offers a build once Beta App Review has passed one (`docs/testflight.md`, *6. Testers*).

2. **Message threads, the latency hunt and Parakeet Ultra** ([#691](https://github.com/samdu/topo/issues/691)). The guest's Claude Code sends its turns on a [message thread](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/message-threads) (beta header `message-threads-2026-08-12`), which holds the conversation on Anthropic's side, so each request sends only the new turn and the time to first token stays low as the conversation grows. Threads are not ZDR-eligible, and the API's compaction cannot run on one: a compaction starts a new thread from its summary. Whether Claude Code in the guest already uses threads, through the proxy, is the first thing to find out. A turn's time is measured with `docs/perf.md`'s marks; the person's save lands ~4 s after the guest hears the words (#355), and a return to the front costs ~10 CPU-s (#501). The ear is Parakeet Ultra (`AsrModelVersion.ultra`, a pinned entry in `models.json`) in place of v2, the spotter and `Vocabulary` unchanged. On Sam's 22 bench recordings through Daphne's path it measured WER 0.027 against v2's 0.059 (samdu/daphne-assistant#205). The encoder is ~595 MB against 442 MB; first load and resident memory on a phone are unmeasured. The ear ships as an Apple-hosted asset pack, as the voice has since #627, so a fresh install has it without the download from Hugging Face after the first launch (about 460 MB today), and a first run can make the ear ready before the voice. Before the pack is built, the model's licence is checked to allow rehosting.
3. **topo-mac** ([#692](https://github.com/samdu/topo/issues/692)). A Mac menu-bar app that runs an always-on guest, which the iPhone uses as a client whenever the Mac is reachable, in place of running its own guest. The hub runs no turns: it holds the lease, shows the pairing code and serves the LAN surfaces, and the phone is the one running the mind. On topo-mac the Mac runs the mind and the phone is its client. When the Mac is out of reach, the phone runs its own guest as it does today. Full Messages access comes with it, since only a Mac can read or send iMessages. So does #597: the phone app run on a Mac draws no tooltips. The architecture with topo-mac in it is the diagram [`roadmap/topo-architecture-mind-and-limbs.webp`](roadmap/topo-architecture-mind-and-limbs.webp): on the iPhone or iPad the mind is local, Claude Code in the guest, or remote, the Mac's mind through the Topo relay; on the Mac Claude Code runs natively; and `topo … --device iphone` lets a central mind use a limb's tools.
4. **topo-fullscreen and panes** ([#693](https://github.com/samdu/topo/issues/693)). The iPad's interface with a tabbed content pane beside the transcript, on either side, and the chat collapsible to a small draggable speech control so the pane can take the screen. In Sam's words of 2026-11-10, the pane is "quite a complex object that people can share over iCloud", and the iPad app should "be able to pop out the pane as a separate window. And even have multiple ones", beside "this full screen iPad built in all one window view". The mockup is [topo.hexagon.zone/fullscreen-mockup](https://topo.hexagon.zone/fullscreen-mockup/).
5. **Full Apple -kit coverage** ([#694](https://github.com/samdu/topo/issues/694)). Sam's principle is that Topo does everything that every app can do, so the kits are worked through one at a time. AlarmKit is the one kit mapped all the way through so far (`topo timer`). Topo also uses ActivityKit, EventKit, HomeKit, MapKit, WidgetKit, RelevanceKit, Contacts and Core Location today, each for what one tool needs. Open in the kits it uses: a Live Activity updated while Topo is suspended, by an APNs sender beside the pane or a `Tracker` record and its silent push, and controls in an activity (#529, whose first step shipped in #557); a widget's action carrying a control's `request` (#283) and running a Shortcut or opening a URL (#284); a translucent or clear tint, and the refusal naming `#RRGGBBAA` (#282, a phone run); Maps results as a card (#358).
6. **topo-watch** ([#695](https://github.com/samdu/topo/issues/695)). The watch is a viewer today. Open on it: the watch's `run` running in place (#327), a slot set from the phone reaching the wrist with the watch app closed on a production build (#525), and the chat read newest-first so the watch is ready before the backlog arrives (#519).

## Parked, and why

The issues here are sub-issues of [#696](https://github.com/samdu/topo/issues/696).

- **What the phone does, made right**, in no step of Sam's order but for what the code audit takes: the `.topo` guard is case-sensitive (#367); a picked file's hidden copy is named by pid alone (#368); a widget cue or a share's file can cross a sign-out (#370, #373); a lease timeout in the preflight fetch escapes as a raw error (`CloudKitRecordDatabase.swift:341`) and has no test (#321; its batch-conflict finding is handled by #306's `mayBeOwnClaim`).
- **Widgets and controls, round two**, in no step of Sam's order but for what is step 5's and step 6's: the slot picker showing each slot's title (#280).
- **A reply on the screen, round two**, in no step of Sam's order: a code block or a table full screen on a tap (#179) and a copy button on code (#217; a turn's menu copies the whole turn).
- **The mind keeps working behind another app**, in no step of Sam's order. A turn sent in front runs on under a continued-processing task (#440); keeping that task alive while the guest's background tasks run is #472.
- **A reply's opening piece at its first comma**, in no step of Sam's order, which takes the ear from Daphne's gains and not this: the piece ends at its first comma, semicolon or dash past 30 characters, not only at a sentence end (samdu/daphne-assistant#207), ready a median 0.28 s sooner on the 9 of 45 turns it changed on Daphne's eval.
- **The guest's harness as one mod**, in no step of Sam's order: everything Topo fits into Claude Code from the inside, the `topo` and `apple-docs` skills, settings, hooks and prompt fragments, as one mod package installed in the guest, the tools staying CLI tools (#490, #535, #536). Sam, 2026-11-04: "everything we need to jimmy into the harness should be in one mod".
- **The emulator's store racing a compile (#425)**, in no step of Sam's order: a store through a writable TLB entry racing a compile that marks the page. Sam chose IC IVAU on 2026-10-23; its PR (#454) is closed unmerged with its branch `buddy/jit-ic-ivau` kept, and no work is under way.
- **Flic, and a standing call.** Daphne's double-press path (samdu/daphne-assistant#324) pairs a Flic through flic2lib, a binary-only xcframework whose licence forbids reverse engineering; Topo is GPL by linking the iSH fork, and the licensing question is Sam's, on the decisions pane. A call that stands between turns, which is what makes a button in a pocket reach Topo, goes with it: today a call exists from the release of a spoken press to its reply's end, and between turns iOS suspends the app by design.
- **Pairing.** The phone's half (`DeviceDirectory.pair`) is built and nothing calls it: no scanner, no `topo://pair` handler (`docs/pairing.md`). Phones registering a `Device` record of their own goes with it; #371 stays open only for a phone reinstalled beside a hub.
- **Sockets and the tunnel.** `TopoLink` carries the hub's lease probe and the LAN surfaces; the phone's probe never confirms, and there are no turn sockets and no tunnel. CloudKit is truth and works with no socket; speed comes after the mind does.
- **An effort selector beside the model's brain.** Each model runs at Anthropic's default effort. Sam's ruling of 2026-11-01: "Let's also park the effort selector on the backlog and just stick to anthropic's default effort levels for each model". Nothing carries an effort to the guest, so building it means the resident's launch and the harness as well as a control in the bar. No issue of its own.

- **The mascot as a movement system (#177)** and the ink he splashes on a correction (#181): the survey of game characters and NPCs comes first and is not begun. Send as a thrown plane Topo catches and reads (#330): the composer it waited on is done (#333), and it waits on Sam to unpark it now that Topo is on TestFlight.

## Explored, not done

- **Picture-in-picture as the way to keep working behind another app**: its tile is opaque, with no transparency.
- **swift-markdown as the parser**: everything a reply draws, tables and links included, comes from Foundation's parser, so nothing on the screen needs the dependency.
- **Kyutai's `english_2026-09_2` weights**: they convert and keep a sentence's opening word, but stop a sentence early at a number written in digits, in Kyutai's own model as in the conversion. They wait on Topo spelling numbers out before synthesis, or on later weights. `english_24l` is not converted.
