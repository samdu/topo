# Topo on the glass

The octopus who roams the chat: the engine, what he shows, where he may stand, how he moves, and the suites that hold it.

Topo himself stands where the look places him (`look.mascot.placement`, `Look.Mascot.Placement`): roaming the chat in whatever gap its words leave him (`roam`, the default), sitting on the composer's glass (`glass`), or at a pin (`pinned`): the octopus drawn in pixels by `Packages/TopoMascot`, Sam's own engine copied unchanged from samdu/experiments `topo-mascot-swift` at 534556a (the Swift port of `topo-mascot-engine`'s JavaScript at f139be1; credited in `THIRD-PARTY`), whose suite holds it to 145 golden frames the pinned JavaScript rendered (`golden.json`, from the experiment's `oracle/golden.mjs`, which stays there), the digest of the whole buffer at chosen frames of every pose, head and load and of the idle cycle, in both facings, and holds the idle cycle's timing and the facing with the experiment's own `RestTests` and `FacingTests`.

Idle, he is the engine's to run: mostly still on the shelf, breathing, blinking and his arms drifting slowly, and now and then an excursion — the engine's stroll to the corner to hang there, which the app gives no length (`corner` 0, since where he is on the screen is the app's and not the engine's), so he hangs where he stands, or, one time in eleven, yoga on his mat — every wait timed from his arrival (20 s before the first, 30–60 s on the shelf between, 6–10 s at the corner, 10–14 s in yoga), and any work calling him home and starting the wait afresh.

The target is built `-O` in every configuration, since at `-Onone` a frame costs three times as much and a debug build is what a device run is.

What he shows is a `MascotState` (`Apps/Client/Mascot.swift`: model, tokens of context, activity, sign, facing) made by a pure function from events, `MascotMapping`: `system/init` and each message's usage name the model and the context, a thinking block is thinking, WebSearch and WebFetch are searching, Bash and a file tool writing code by its extension are building, one writing anything else, the memory included, is writing, a tool that says nothing about the work leaves the pose alone, and nothing is calendar; a turn's start and its end — answered, failed, abandoned, or its updates stopping with none — are idle, so no pose outlives its turn. Yoga is not an activity: it is the idle cycle's, and nothing the app hands the engine asks for it. The app holds one `Mascot`, in the environment; the chat's harness hands it the model it asks and the context its last reply reported (`Harness.context`, only this phone's own replies; none, which is an empty context, until this login's first reply, since a sign-out takes the last one's with it), and between the guest's turns no pose; during one, the guest's own events move him (`Mascot.follow`, handed each `GuestActivity` the bridge reports through `Harness.onGuest`, which `Mascot.follow(_ harness:)` installs in `TopoApp`; `Harness.guestBrain` is the bridge and its relay, as `Harness.standard` composes them), as they do in `DebugRun.guestTurn`.

Where he is drawn is `Apps/Client/MascotView.swift`, laid over the whole of the chat (`mascotRoams`, an `overlayPreferenceValue` on the chat's content, under the navigation bar and down through the composer), so he is drawn above everything in the chat — the transcript, the lines under it and the offer card — and only the keyboard is above him, while the composer's pane is somewhere he never stands roaming; he takes no room, no touch but the long press that picks him up (below) and is nothing to accessibility; the composer draws only its own controls.

What he stands clear of is reported by the views that draw it, as anchors into one preference, `MascotScene` (`Apps/Client/MascotRoost.swift`): the transcript's frame (`mascotVisible`), every enclosed turn's enclosure and time before the row takes the column's width, each line of a turn with nothing drawn round its words — Topo's, at the default look, a line of each block of his markdown, a list item's marker included — and its time on its own (`mascotLines`, `Look.Enclosure.drawsNothing`: a wrapped `Text` reports a frame as wide as its widest line, so the lines are read from its `Text.Layout` through a `TextRenderer` that draws them unchanged, each line's typographic bounds, empty lines left out; iOS 18, and the whole frame on iOS 17 and until the first draw), so the room after a short line is room, a code block's enclosure, a quote's bar and a rule whole (`MarkdownText`), the row at the end of the transcript, the notice, the lines under the transcript — the status, the error, the waiting and ask-again controls — and the offer card (`mascotObstacle`), and the composer's pane and well (`mascotPane`, `mascotWell`); the overlay resolves them in its own space into a `MascotField`, with the keyboard's frame while it is up.

His box is `MascotSprite.box` (33, 67, 94×89), the part of the engine's 184×160 picture he takes up at rest: sitting on the shelf at home, breathing, blinking, looking about and his arms drifting, on every head and load band and in both facings, with two pixels round (`MascotGeometryTests.testHeRestsInsideTheBox` runs the idle state and holds the box is exactly that; `box × look.mascot.scale` is what a gap has to hold, and the box is all the words are judged against, for the clearance, the hurry and "covered". The canvas draws the whole picture round the box, so an excursion or a working pose reaches past it over the words; they are brief, and the words answer none of it. The screen's edge, the pane, the well and the keyboard do: `MascotSprite.reach` (15, 47, 130×113) is every pose the app can ask for — the idle cycle with its corner and yoga, the walk, every working pose, on every head and load band and the ways between them, in both facings, with two pixels round (`testEveryPoseTheAppAsksForIsDrawnInsideTheReach` runs a schedule of them every frame, holds it, and records each pose's reach past the box) — and the box stands only where that reach is inside the transcript's frame and above the pane's top edge and the keyboard's (`MascotField.room`), and keeps from the pane, the well and the keyboard the clearance or the reach, whichever is longer on each side (`MascotField.kept`), so no prop is cut off by the edge or drawn on the glass at any clearance, 0 included: 18 art pixels either side, 20 up and 4 down, times the scale. Facing right the engine mirrors the picture about his body's axis, so both are the union of the two facings, symmetric about that axis: his body stands at the box's centre whichever way he faces, and a turn moves nothing on the screen. "Covered", the mid-glide re-decision and placing him at once judge the pane, the well and the keyboard by the reach for the same reason. The sign is not in the reach, since nothing in the app sets `MascotState.sign`; it would reach about 40 pixels further out on the side he faces.

Where he stands is a pure function, `MascotRoost.decide` (`MascotRoost.of` is its roost): a gap is a place in the transcript's frame, drawn in by his reach, above the pane's top edge and the keyboard's where his picture with `look.mascot.clearance` all round it overlaps nothing he may not cover — the clearance kept from what he may not cover and not from the transcript's own edges, so the reply margin with the column's padding (126 points on a 393-point phone) holds his picture at a scale of 1 with its reach inside the screen's edge, and the ends of short lines add to it — each obstacle as far as it can be seen (from the transcript's top edge down to the pane's and the keyboard's, so a turn under the glass or the bar is not one and a line under the transcript is), the whole of the pane and the well, at every presence, keyboard up or down, and the keyboard; of the gaps, the nearest to where he stands wins, so a new turn moves him the least, and of two as near the one further right, and the nearest to the transcript's bottom trailing corner, the right margin just above the glass, before he has stood anywhere. With no gap he still stands, where the least of his box is over the words (the area of it over them as far as they can be seen, which is none where the least covered place is only within his clearance of a word), with the same nearest-then-right rule between places that cover as little: obscuring a line is better than disappearing. The pane, the well, the keyboard and the screen's edge go on holding his reach whatever the words leave, so the glass is never a roost, a fallback or a waiting place; where the clearance kept from the pane, the well and the keyboard leaves no place at all, they keep his reach alone (`Decision.relaxed`), which the bottom of the room always leaves him (`testWhereTheClearanceFromTheGlassLeavesNoPlaceHisReachAloneIsKept`: a scale of 1, a clearance of 32, the keyboard up and 120 to 140 points above the pane). Every place is weighed on one lattice — the aim's own x and y, the room's edges, every edge of what the words, the pane, the well and the keyboard forbid, and the edges at which his box starts and stops overlapping each word, which is where the covered area, bilinear between them, is least — so the least covered place is the least covered anywhere and no search is added for it. He stands nowhere only where no place exists at all: the transcript still to be read, a placed policy with no pane or no transcript, a size that is not one, and a room his reach cannot fit (the `full` transcript at a scale of 4).

The decision is a value, `MascotRoost.Decision`: the field whole (`MascotField` is `Codable`), his size, clearance and reach, the room, the aim, the one chosen with the area of it over words and whether it clears, whether the pane, the well and the keyboard kept his reach alone, and in a debug build every place weighed, which a release build does not keep. `MascotRoam` keeps the last one it made (`decision`), a debug build's report carries it (`MascotRoam.Report.decision`: the whole of it in the app, and in the chat's JSON the chosen frame, whether it clears, its cost, the aim and how many places were weighed and cleared), and the trace writes it as a line of its own each time one is made, so what the overlay draws, what the report says and what a trace replays are one value. The roam can keep him where he stands within his clearance of the place chosen, where that is a gap as it stands, and where no place clears, where he stands for a place chosen that uncovers no more than a tenth of his box beyond it, so the chosen frame and his frame differ only then, mid-glide, and while he rides the words, where the decision is still where it was made. A placed Topo, on the glass or at a pin, keeps no decision, and the overlay draws none for him.

A debug build draws his field over the chat with the settings sheet's Tuning switch `Show his field` on (the defaults key `topo.debug.mascotField`, off by default, which a launch sets for one run with the argument `-topo.debug.mascotField YES`): `MascotFieldOverlay` (`Apps/Client/MascotDebugOverlay.swift`), a `Canvas` in `MascotLayer`'s own stack, so it takes no touch and is nothing to accessibility, drawing the words as he read them, the room, every place weighed outlined as one path with the ones that clear filled as one shape, the one chosen, and his box where he is now with his reach round it, in the colours and widths of `look.mascot.debug`. It draws the decision the report carries and computes nothing.

Which way he faces is decided with each roost that is a gap (`MascotRoam.facing`): `MascotFacing.of` puts the roost's centre, which is his body's axis, against the transcript's vertical midline, right of it `right` — the engine draws him mirrored, the sign held out to the left — and on it or left of it the picture as drawn; the canvas hands a changed facing to `Mascot.facing` (`mascotRoams`' `face`), which the events and the harness never touch and which reaches the engine in every input, so a glide across the line turns him once, at the decision that sent him, and the engine takes it when he is next at rest at home. An empty chat and a full one both put him right of the line. The chat's debug report (`DebugRun.ChatReport.facing`) carries the facing.

Going from roost to roost is `MascotRoam`, a value the canvas moves on with its own clock — real time from the display link's timestamps, at most two frame intervals (or a tenth of a second, whichever is longer) a callback so a stall moves him on by that and no more — and hands each new geometry, two decisions kept apart: where to go is decided once the geometry has held still for `roamSettle`, since the transcript reports its geometry on every frame of a scroll — or for one frame while something is over him that he did not choose, so a covered Topo gets out of the way as soon as the scroll or a landing turn stops — never mid-glide, except that a geometry arriving mid-glide that leaves where he is going no roost, or puts the pane, the well or the keyboard on the rest of his way, turns the glide at once from where he is, while one that leaves the destination a roost and the way clear keeps it; a geometry that leaves any of him below the pane's top edge or the keyboard's — the glass rising with the keyboard onto or past him, which SwiftUI lays out in one step while the render server animates it across him — places him at once with no glide, since a glide out from under it would cross the glass; and a roost within his clearance of where he stands is not a move while where he stands is a roost itself (`MascotRoost.holds`), and one that is not moves however short the move; until the transcript has been read once (`Harness.hasRead`, handed to the overlay as `ready`: a read that returned, with turns or with none, and never one that threw, so a launch with no connection keeps him undrawn until a read gets through) every decision is nowhere, and he is not drawn, so a page about to fill is not where he is put, and the read counts as a geometry change, so his first decision where to stand is a settle after it; the first geometry to hold still places him with no glide; a look changing his scale or clearance decides again at once with no glide, whether he stands or glides, and a settle later while he stands nowhere, so he is never drawn at a size or clearance no roost was decided for, and Reduce Motion coming on ends a glide at its destination.

A move is one glide at `roamSpeed` points a second on average, eased at both ends — `hurry` times that on every frame anything he may not cover overlaps his picture, a turn or a line under the transcript, and back to the stroll the frame he is clear (the ease is one curve run faster, `Move.elapsed` counted in the stroll's seconds) — and under Reduce Motion a placement with no glide.

A word that displaces him sends him to its far side from where it is going — under a word moving up the screen or standing still, so of room above and below a still turn below wins, and over one moving down — so it passes over him once rather than pushing him ahead of it; where a glide is going moves with the words as they scroll (`MascotField.drift`: between two geometries, the move most of the obstacles that moved agree on, pairing each with those of its size, unrounded so a drag of a tenth of a point a geometry adds up; none when more obstacles stood still than agree on it, and none when the transcript's frame, the pane, the well or the keyboard moved), and which way the words are going is the last geometry's move alone, so a turn landing just after a scroll is a still one, so a decision taken mid-scroll is still a gap on the next frame and a crossing is one decision; a roost within his clearance of the one a glide was going to carries that glide on, timed afresh from where he is; with no room on the far side he goes to the near one and rides the words (`MascotRoam.riding`) until they settle or carry him to the edge of his room, where the drift stops taking him, so he is never drawn past it, the ride ends and he glides on from inside it. A frame's quiet is judged to three quarters of the frame interval, since the display link's timestamps and the clock's sums put it a hair either side.

A debug build launched with `TOPO_DEBUG_MASCOT_TRACE=<name>` writes every geometry the canvas hands the roam, every tick, with where it left him, and every decision whole, as JSON lines to that name in the app's temporary directory (`MascotTrace`), which is how a run is replayed in a test. `MascotCrossingTests` replays two windows of such a run (`Tests/Client/Traces/`, the `full` fixture with the person's inset at 0, so a person's turn spans the column, dragged slowly on the simulator: one descending onto him and springing back, and one rising into him with the glass over its foot, which he rides above to the top of his room and then passes under once) and scripts the rest: one glide a crossing, never standing nowhere, never standing under text past the one tick before a decision, no glide ending under text, the side he ends on, under a rising turn, over a descending one, below a still one with room either side and one landing just after a scroll, above one rising out of the glass with no room under it, riding the words, and the drift carried past a still obstacle of a bubble's size and a tenth of a point a geometry; and a third recording (`fling.json`, the `full` fixture flung up and down at 1,200 to 5,000 points a second) and a ride at a quarter of a point a geometry hold every tick inside his room with no move but a glide or the words' own.

Whether anything he may not cover overlaps his picture where it is (`MascotRoam.covered`) is judged on every geometry and every frame with no wait, and never stops him being drawn; he is not drawn only while he stands nowhere. Standing over words because nothing clears them, he is covered for as long as he stands there, so a glide out, when room opens, runs at the hurry from its first frame; a geometry change is decided at the settle, as for a Topo standing clear, since a frame's quiet would weigh every place again on nearly every frame of a reply growing under him. The place chosen then moves him only where it uncovers more than a tenth of his box beyond where he stands (`MascotRoost.fallbackGain`) or clears the words; otherwise he stays where he is while it is a place at all. A glide cut short is not standing, and goes on to the place chosen. A glide to a place over words keeps going while that place is still a place at all (`MascotRoost.admits`: in his room, his reach clear of the pane, the well and the keyboard), and the settle after it decides again. While he glides he wears the engine's walk; on arrival he wears the activity he stands for, never idle for its own sake.

The other two placements are `MascotPerch`, pure functions of the same field, and the roam places him by them rather than searching for a gap: nothing is over him, since the words and the glass are not obstacles there. His first place waits, as a roaming Topo's does, for the transcript to have been read and the geometry to have held still for a settle, and is a placement, at once, with no glide: the geometries of a chat still laying itself out place him nowhere. On `glass` he sits in the composer's empty flank — the trailing one, the leading one holding the keyboard's control — with the engine's shelf on the pane's top edge and his body in the middle of the flank between the well's trailing edge and the pane's end, drawn inside that flank and nothing of it over the well (`MascotPerch.glassSlot`, the canvas's clip), riding the pane wherever it goes, under the keyboard included: standing there he is drawn in `MascotGlassStage`, a view the size of his whole picture that SwiftUI places from the pane's own anchor inside a clip of the flank, so the stage and the clip are animated in the transaction the pane is — the keyboard's, on its curve — and he rides the pane as drawn rather than being put at its end; a debug build's report carries, frame by frame while anything moves, the top of his picture and the keyboard's top edge as drawn (`MascotCanvas.trail`, the presentation layers), which `TopoPlacementTests` holds him to through a rise and a fall; and the pane is drawn whole at every presence (`PanePresence.of`'s `holdsTopo`), since a Topo on invisible glass is a Topo floating. On `pinned` his box's centre is put at `look.mascot.pin`, a fraction across and down the transcript's frame carried down to the pane's foot (`MascotField.pinFrame`, the lines under the transcript and the glass being part of the chat a person can put him over) with the keyboard down (`MascotRoam.resting`, the last such frame of a geometry with no keyboard in it), moved in only as far as keeps his reach inside that frame (`MascotPerch.inside`); the keyboard coming up over him lifts him clear of it, keeping his clearance or his reach from its top edge, at the hurry, and its going sends him back to the pin at the stroll, the pin itself never rewritten. A change of policy or of pin is one glide at the stroll from where he stands to where the new one puts him, and from a pin to `roam` a roost is decided from where he stands; under Reduce Motion it is a placement. Beyond those, and a cancelled press taking him back (below), nothing glides him once placed: any other geometry moves him with it at once, and a new size or clearance places him at once, as it does roaming (`MascotRoam.Arrival`). He faces the half of the transcript his box's centre is in under every placement.

A long press on him and a drag pins him: `MascotCanvas.grab`, a `UILongPressGestureRecognizer` (half a second, ten points of slop) on the window the canvas is in, handed only touches on his box less the well's frame (`MascotRoam.grabbable`) while he is drawn with no sheet over the chat, so a scroll that starts on him moves off before it fires and scrolls, a tap on him is whatever is under him's, and the well keeps its press whatever is drawn over it; it waits on nothing, and every other recognizer but a pan — any `UIPanGestureRecognizer`, a scroll's among them — waits for it to fail. The finger carries him at his size (`MascotRoam.grab`, `drag(to:)`, his reach held inside the frame), with nothing decided on the way, and letting go (`drop`) pins him where he is: the roam is `pinned` at that pin from that moment, and the chat hands the pin to `Tuning.pin(at:)`, which writes `placement: pinned` and `pin` to this device's override, so a pin outlives a relaunch and a vault's `look.json` does not undo it. A press let go where it picked him up moved him nowhere and pins nothing, so a pin at the frame's very edge is not rewritten to where the edge held him. A press cancelled rather than let go — a call, an alert, the system taking the touch — pins nothing and hands nothing on: he goes back to where his policy puts him, with a glide (`MascotRoam.cancelDrag`). Under Reduce Motion the drag works and he is the still he always is. The chat's debug report carries his placement, whether a finger has him and how many drags have begun (`MascotRoam.Report`).

`look.mascot` (`Look.Mascot`) is `placement`, one of `roam`, `glass` and `pinned`; `pin`, an `x` and a `y` each 0–1, refused whole when either is out of range or missing; `scale`, 0.25–4, nearest-neighbour, 1 by default, his original size; `clearance`, 0–64 points; `roamSpeed`, 10–400 points a second; `hurry`, 1–20 times it, 10 by default; `roamSettle`, 0.1–5 s; and `frameInterval`, 1/120–1 s; and `debug`, the overlay of his field: seven colours (`box`, `reach`, `room`, `candidate`, `clearing`, `chosen`, `words`) and two widths (`lineWidth`, `hairline`), 0.1–8 points.

`MascotDriver` steps the engine and draws a frame only while he animates — the scene active, the canvas in a window, drawn at any opacity above zero (the microphone's hold fades him with the glass's flanks to `composer.flank.heldOpacity`, and frames stop only when that is zero), nothing covering the chat (the settings, diagnostics and memory sheets), and him standing somewhere — and the canvas's `CADisplayLink` exists only while he animates or, with the chat in front, his roam has a glide or a decision waiting on the clock; a Topo standing nowhere with nothing to decide asks for nothing. Under Reduce Motion he is one still of the idle pose, drawn again only for what the engine draws differently, a new head, load band or facing (`MascotDriver.Still`), and not for tokens moving within one.

Frames are drawn on the main thread, update and draw of a 184×160 buffer and a `CGImage` handed to one layer, which a debug build times whole and prints every 300 frames as `[topo-debug] mascot: …`, with the facing in force (`MascotDriver.frameLine`): 1.0 to 3.0 ms mean, p95 up to 4.1 ms, and 15.4 ms worst while the guest was starting, in a simulator on buddybox, against the 33 ms a frame has at the default interval. His colours are the engine's own teal, and no theme recolours him.
