# The mind on the hub

With the menu's switch on ("Run the mind on this Mac", off by default, kept in the hub's defaults as `mind.on` on the real store only), the hub holds the primary lease while the Mac is awake and answers the person's turns from the CloudKit log through a resident native Claude Code (`NativeBrain`). The phone writes its turn to the log as a limb, defers to the hub's fresh lease (`HubLeaseProbe`, `docs/harness.md`) and draws the reply. Off, the hub claims nothing and runs no mind; the pairing code, the device list and the LAN surfaces carry on either way. The loop is `HubMind` (`Packages/TopoMind/Sources/TopoMind/HubMind.swift`), which `HubModel` (`Apps/TopoHub/HubModel.swift`) makes once the probe listener's endpoint is known and drives from the switch, the workspace's sleep and wake, the push and quit.

## The lease

The hub takes the lease from whoever holds it (`PrimaryLease.takeOver()`) once each time the mind starts: the switch turned on, the hub launched with it on, each wake from sleep, and Try again after a stop. A takeover that cannot be sent is sent again, backing off to 30 s, until it is answered. From then on the lease is kept by its own heartbeats, which every answered write starts (5 s, a 10 s duration), and by the runner's writes, each a batch with a heartbeat. Before every pass a lease this Mac does not hold — lapsed after late heartbeats, or taken by a phone — is asked for with `acquire()`, never `takeOver()`. A phone that claimed over the hub's live lease is a holder the hub yielded to at its next heartbeat or batch, and keeps the lease until it lapses: it is not claimed back on a timer, and the two do not flip. A phone's lease the hub has not yielded to is another matter: the phone answers no probe (its lease names no endpoint, so `SocketLeaseProbe` says no), so `acquire()` claims over it on that one probe, as it would over any holder it cannot reach. That is the hub whose own lease lapsed (late heartbeats, a network gap) and which a phone claimed meanwhile: the hub takes it back at its next pass, and the phone yields at its next heartbeat. Every stop is `PrimaryLease.abandon()`, which stops the heartbeats at once and makes any write sent after it hold nothing; the record lapses one duration later and the phone claims it at its next turn or pass.

## Starting and stopping

A start goes in this order, so the lease is never held by a mind that cannot answer: the mind's home is made (`MindHome.prepare`), Claude Code is asked whether it is signed in (`MindLogin.check`), the resident process is started, the runner is made (its writer is this Mac's, read from the log), then the lease is taken over and the first pass runs.

- **Switch off**: the pass under way is cancelled, the lease abandoned, the session waited for, and the process shut down and reaped before the call returns. The runner checks cancellation before each append, and the abandoned lease refuses a batch, so nothing is written after the switch.
- **Will-sleep** (`NSWorkspace.willSleepNotification`): the same, but the process is left; the Mac suspends it with everything else. The notification's handler waits up to 2 s for the stop, so the lease is abandoned before the system goes on to sleep. A turn the sleep cut off is listened to again on the same process at wake rather than asked twice. Nothing runs while asleep, a dark wake's timers included, because the loop is gone.
- **Did-wake** (`NSWorkspace.didWakeNotification`): a start, with its one takeover.
- **Quit** (`applicationShouldTerminate`): as switch off, with the switch kept.
- **Signed out, or a mind that cannot run**: a login that says signed out, a turn that fails `NativeBrainError.notSignedIn`, a process that cannot be started, a ledger that cannot be read, or a home that cannot be made stops the mind as switch off does and leaves it stopped, saying why, until Try again, the switch, or a wake starts it again. The phone answers once the lease lapses. A turn that ends `unresolved` or `failed` is a failed pass, not a stop; an `unresolved` turn waits for the person, as on the phone.

## Triggers

A pass is `TurnRunner.answerPending`. One runs at a time: on a trigger, and every 10 s (`HubMind.standardPoll`) as the backstop. A trigger while the loop waits starts the pass at once; any number of triggers before it begins are that one pass, and any number during a pass are one more pass after it, since the pass may have read the log before their turn landed. A pass with nothing waiting is one read of the log (the zone's change feed and a fetch by ID of each device's next sequence number) and touches neither the lease nor the brain, which is the same read the phone's 5 s loop makes; the hub's interval is twice the phone's because the push is the fast path.

The push is `HubPush` (`Apps/TopoHub/HubPush.swift`): a `CKQuerySubscription` on `Turn` creation in the `Topo` zone, content-available only, `sequence > 0` (for the reason `docs/harness.md` gives for the phone's), saved under its own id, `hub-turn-created`, against the real store only and once a launch with the switch on. The hub never saves or reads the phone's `turn-created`: subscriptions are kept in the Apple ID's private database, so the id is what tells the two apart. `HubAppDelegate` registers for remote notifications at launch (silent pushes ask no permission), logs whether APNs registered (`push` category, never the token), and hands a push that is the hub's subscription's to `HubModel.pushed()`, which triggers a pass. The push carries nothing; the turn is read from the log as always.

## The mind's home

The mind has none of the Mac user's Claude Code: its working directory is `~/Library/Application Support/Topo Hub/home` and its `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` `~/Library/Application Support/Topo Hub/claude` (`MindHome`), both made at each start. Claude Code reads `CLAUDE.md`, `CLAUDE.local.md` and `.claude/CLAUDE.md` in every directory above the working directory, so from there it would read `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` as a project's memory; `MindHome.prepare` merges a `claudeMdExcludes` entry for each of those, in every directory above, into the config directory's `settings.json` and leaves the rest of the file as it is (a file that is not a JSON object is refused, not overwritten). `claude -p /context` run from the home with that config directory lists no memory files. The process runs with permission mode `auto`, its environment the few variables `NativeBrain` inherits and a `PATH` of Homebrew's prefixes, `~/.local/bin` and the system's (`MindHome.path`); `claude` is `~/.local/bin/claude`.

The login is Claude Code's own, made by hand in that config directory: `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=~/Library/Application\ Support/Topo\ Hub/claude ~/.local/bin/claude auth login --claudeai`. The menu shows the command when the mind is signed out. Whether it is signed in is `claude auth status --json` with that config directory, run off the main thread with a 20 s bound: the method and the subscription, never the credential.

## The model

The model is a menu picker of the phone's choices, Sonnet, Opus and Fable, Sonnet by default, kept in the hub's defaults under `model` as the phone keeps its own (`Harness.modelKey`): the choice is each device's. Every request carries it and `NativeBrain` applies `ClaudeModel.effective`, so a Debug build answers with Haiku whatever the picker says, and the menu says so; the signed build `scripts/hub-dev-build.sh` makes is Release and unpinned.

## The menu

The mind's section shows the switch, the picker, one line on where the mind stands (starting, answering and whether this Mac holds the lease, asleep, or why it stopped, with Try again), the mind's own line (`NativeBrain.describe()`: its process, model and session), and the last pass's outcome, time and trigger. The status line above it says whether this Mac is primary or who is, from the mind's lease and a read of the lease record on the hub's 10 s refresh.

## Timing a pass

Each pass writes `Perf` marks (`perf` category, the `zone.hexagon.topo` subsystem): `hub.pass.trigger <start|push|poll>`, then, with the milliseconds since the trigger, `hub.pass.read` (the runner's read of the log answered), `hub.pass.lease` (the lease's write before the brain is asked), `hub.pass.asked` with `turn=`, the milliseconds since the newest turn it answers was said, `hub.pass.answered`, `hub.pass.saved` (the reply's batch answered), and `hub.pass.end <outcome>`. A push landing is `hub.push.received`; takeovers, acquires and stops are `hub.lease.takeOver`, `hub.lease.acquire` and `hub.mind.stopped`, each with its outcome's name. The runner's stages are read off the calls it makes through the database and the brain the hub hands it (`TimedDatabase`, `TimedBrain`, `PassClock.swift`). No mark carries a word of a turn, and no failure's mark carries the strings Claude Code gave.

## Against nothing real

`TOPO_STORE=memory` puts even a signed hub on the in-memory store, and the hub's first log line says which store it is on (`hub store memory`). On that store alone the mind's home is `~/Library/Application Support/Topo Hub (memory store)`, the switch is never kept, and three variables and two signals drive a run: `TOPO_HUB_MIND=on` turns the mind on, `TOPO_HUB_CLAUDE` names the `claude` it runs (the suites' fake, `Packages/TopoMind/Tests/TopoMindTests/Fixtures/fake-claude`, behind a wrapper that sets its `FAKE_LOG`), `TOPO_HUB_LIMB_SAYS` writes a limb's turn at launch, SIGUSR1 flips the switch as the menu does, and SIGUSR2 posts the workspace's will-sleep and did-wake in turn. No push subscription is saved there.

## Tests

`swift test` in `Packages/TopoMind` runs `HubMindTests` against `InMemoryRecordDatabase` and the fake `claude`, with a probe that confirms every holder, so only a takeover can move a fresh lease: off claims nothing; signed out at the switch claims nothing; on takes over once, answers a limb's turns, and does not take the lease back from a phone that claimed it; off mid-turn writes no reply and leaves no process; sleep stops the renewals and wake takes over once; a turn that finds Claude Code signed out stops the loop and lets the lease lapse; triggers at once run one pass and never two together.
